Nocturne structure · Batsu brand colors

Design system

Same system, same geometry — the accent role now carries Batsu’s magenta, with the logo’s cyan as the secondary field. Unity reads them as literal px at 1920×1080 and doubles cleanly to 4K.

Palette

Dark near-neutral ground, one accent voice. The accent is a line and a glow — never a flood.

accent
100 → 900. Steps 700–900 for tinted fills, hovers and hairlines on this ground · 500 base · 100–300 for text on those tints. Text on ground is #f0e9f2; muted text is the same at 55%.

Type

Inter, weights 400/500/600 — one family, hierarchy from size and space. Convert Inter-Regular / Inter-Medium / Inter-SemiBold to TMP assets; nothing heavier than 600.
